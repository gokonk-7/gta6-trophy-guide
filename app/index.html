    Road to the platinum

    Three phases, built from the official descriptions. Roughly 200 hours in total — the story first, then the 100% grind, then the strangers and clean-up.

    Missables & save discipline

    There is no mission replay and no New Game+ in GTA VI. Almost everything can be done after the story — except, possibly, one meditation spot.

    MISSABLE? Positive Mental Attitude

    Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, on the morning after the mission Down to Earth, disappears after Chapter 1. It is not yet confirmed whether that spot is required for the trophy's 18 spots — so do it the first chance you get. Meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice, and the last spot needs both characters, possible after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. Track the count under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations.

    TIME-SENSITIVE Yago's race

    Yago's Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 shows as an hourglass icon and counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Do it the moment it appears — and save first.

    PERMANENT Criminal profile

    An irredeemable criminal profile is permanent. Actions Speak Louder needs 500 Criminal Profile actions, but do not push toward irredeemable on purpose — most players reach 500 just by playing.

    SAVE Save discipline

    You get 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start of every chapter, and one more from just before the final mission. That is your only safety net: there is no mission replay.
