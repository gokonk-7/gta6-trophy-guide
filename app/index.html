    Roadmap to the platinum

    Three phases, built from the official trophy descriptions. Play the story first, grind the 100% checklist second, and finish the stranger strands and one-offs last. About 200 hours in all — no difficulty trophies, no mission replay, no New Game+, so save discipline matters more than speed.

    Missables & save discipline

    Only one trophy is possibly missable — but the game's lack of mission replay and New Game+ makes your saves your only safety net.

    Missable Positive Mental Attitude

    Lucia can meditate on her balcony the morning after “Down to Earth” in Chapter 1, and that spot disappears after Chapter 1. Whether the Chapter 1 spot is required is not yet confirmed — do it while you can and do not risk it. Meditate at 18 spots in total; meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice, and the last spot needs both characters (possible after Coffee Break in Chapter 2). Track it under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations.

    Sources: GTABase, Outputlag.

    Time-sensitive Yago’s race — Strangers & Freaks

    Yago’s Race Against Yago appears in Chapter 3 with an hourglass icon and counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Do it the moment it appears, and save first.

    Sources: GTABase, Outputlag.

    Permanent Irredeemable criminal profile

    Your Criminal Profile is permanent — pushing toward “irredeemable” cannot be undone. Actions Speak Louder (500 profile actions) arrives through normal play; do not chase it deliberately. Turn on Criminal Profile notifications in Settings > UI to watch the count.

    Sources: GTABase, Outputlag.

    Save discipline

    15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start of every chapter, plus one right before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them on a platinum run.

    Sources: GTABase, Outputlag.
