    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions. Three phases, in order — finish the story first, then the 100% checklist, then the strangers and clean-up.

    Phase 1 — Story

    Play the story, save at the start of every chapter. This phase covers the 12 story trophies (chapter completions and mission completions from Gone Fishing to Operation Savage Glory) plus the final mission.

    Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude before Chapter 1 ends — the only possibly-missable trophy in the game.

      Phase 2 — 100% checklist

      The grind behind A Long Road: 250 stunts (300 for 100%), gold at all 42 Shooting Range challenges, 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, 15 Target Decks, 200 vehicle scans, 100 animal studies, 100 WAiNK scans, 70 of 80 challenges. Work it after the story, when the whole map is open.

        Phase 3 — Strangers & clean-up

        Eight stranger strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago, Pilot Pete and the rest), the six side-story finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), the one-off trophies, and the money goals — $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all garages, 10 Scooter Bros registrations.

          Missables & save discipline

          ⚠ Positive Mental Attitude — the only possibly-missable trophy

          Meditate at 18 spots across Leonida. Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, the morning after “Down to Earth”, disappears after Chapter 1 — do it before the chapter ends. Meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice, and the last spot needs both characters (possible after Coffee Break in Chapter 2). Track it under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations. It is not yet confirmed whether the Chapter 1 spot is required — treat it as required.

          ⏱ Yago's race — time-sensitive, not missable

          Yago's Race Against Yago (Chapter 3, hourglass icon) counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Do it the moment it appears, and save first.

          ⚠ Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent

          Actions Speak Louder needs 500 Criminal Profile actions — most players get it without trying. Do not push toward an irredeemable profile deliberately; it cannot be undone.

          Save discipline

          There is no mission replay and no New Game+, and cheats disable trophies and saving. You get 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave — keep one save at the start of every chapter, plus one before the final mission. That is your only safety net for the missable meditation and the time-sensitive race.

          Missable trophies in this guide: 1 flagged (Positive Mental Attitude). Every other trophy can be earned after the story.
