    The road to platinum

    Around 200 hours, in three phases. The story first, then the 100% checklist, then the strangers and the clean-up. Nothing on this road is missable except one meditation — see Missables.

    A Long Road — the 100% checklist

    Required in full: all robberies (8 bank vaults, 39 gang operations), underwater discoveries, every Shooting Range gold medal, all drone races, Bret's 15 Target Decks. Only a share of the rest: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 100 WAiNK scans. The checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story — everything you did before already counts.

    Missables & save discipline

    Good news: GTA VI is forgiving. There is one trophy that may be missable, one time-sensitive race, and one permanent choice to avoid.

    Save discipline

    You get 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so a chapter-start save is your only way back. Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them on a platinum run.

    What to do next, in order. Time-sensitive things first, then the story order, then the grind — so you never have to wonder what is worth doing now.
