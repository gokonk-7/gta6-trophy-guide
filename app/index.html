    The road to the platinum

    About 200 hours, no difficulty trophies, no mission replay and no New Game+ — so play the story once, save often, and clean up afterwards. Three phases, in order.

    Missables & save discipline

    ⚠ Positive Mental Attitude — the only possibly-missable trophy

    Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, on the morning after Down to Earth, disappears after Chapter 1. Meditate at 18 spots across Leonida; meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice, and the last spot needs both characters (possible after Coffee Break in Chapter 2). Track it under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations. It is not yet confirmed whether the Chapter 1 spot is required — treat it as required and do it early.

    ⏰ Yago's race — time-sensitive, not missable yet

    Race Against Yago appears in Chapter 3 with an hourglass icon and counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Do it the moment it appears, and save first.

    🚨 Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent

    Actions Speak Louder needs 500 Criminal Profile actions, and most players get it without trying. Do not push toward an Irredeemable profile on purpose — that rating does not come back.

    Save discipline

    15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start of every chapter, and one more from just before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them alone on a trophy run.
