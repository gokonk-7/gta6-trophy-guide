    The road to platinum

    Three phases, in order. Play the story first and save at every chapter start — there is no mission replay and no New Game+.

    Missables & save discipline

    ⚠ The only possibly-missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots). Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, on the morning after Down to Earth, disappears after Chapter 1, and it is not yet confirmed whether that spot is required. Do it before Chapter 1 ends. Meditating with Jason and Lucia together counts twice; the last spot needs both characters (possible after Coffee Break, Ch. 2). Track it under Progress > Collections > Exploration > Meditations.

    ⏱ Time-sensitive: Yago's race

    Yago's Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 counts toward Strangers & Freaks. Do it the moment its hourglass icon appears — and save first.

    ⛔ Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent

    Don't push your Criminal Profile toward irredeemable just to chase the Actions Speak Louder counter (500 actions). Most players reach it without trying.

    💾 Save discipline

    15 manual save slots + 1 autosave. Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one right before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving — don't use them on a trophy run.
